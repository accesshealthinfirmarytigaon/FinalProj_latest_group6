Part I

Consent Form

All respondents were required to review and agree to the following consent items before participating in the evaluation. Participation was entirely voluntary and respondents could withdraw at any time.

Study Description

This study evaluates the usability of the Tigaon – Primary Hospital and Clinic Website to assess how easily users can navigate the platform and access health-related services. As a participant, respondents browsed the website, explored key features (e.g., appointment scheduling, health service directories), submitted a sample inquiry, and completed the System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire.

Duration: Approximately 8–10 minutes. Participation was entirely voluntary. All responses were kept strictly confidential, anonymized, and used solely to improve the platform.

Consent Items

  • ✓ I understand the purpose of this study and voluntarily agree to participate in the usability evaluation of the Tigaon Infirmary – Community Hospital and Clinic Website.
  • ✓ I consent to the collection and use of my feedback and evaluation responses solely for the purpose of improving the Tigaon Infirmary – Community Hospital and Clinic Website platform.
  • ✓ I agree that anonymized data from my participation may be included in academic reports or publications related to this project.
  • ✓ I acknowledge that I may withdraw my consent and discontinue participation at any time without any negative consequences.
  • ✓ I agree to allow documentation (such as photographs) during the evaluation session for the sole purpose of academic reporting by the student group.

Part III

Survey Instruments

The following tools were used to collect evaluation data from respondents. Each instrument was explained prior to use.

System Usability Scale (SUS) Questionnaire

The SUS is a widely used, reliable tool for measuring perceived usability. It consists of 10 alternating positive/negative statements rated on a 5-point Likert scale. The final score ranges from 0–100, with higher scores indicating better usability.

Heuristic Evaluation Workbook

Each group member conducted an independent heuristic evaluation using Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics as the framework. Each evaluator identified usability problems, rated their severity, and noted which heuristic was violated.
